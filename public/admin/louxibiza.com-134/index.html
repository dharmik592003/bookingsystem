F.A.Q
How does the booking process work?
To successfully confirm your luxury villa rental in Ibiza, simply send us your villa rental request, and we'll handpick exclusive villa options tailored to your preferences. Once you've chosen your desired option, provide us with a copy of your passport so we can prepare your rental contract.

Confirm your booking by paying a 50% deposit, securing your chosen villa for your desired dates. The remaining balance is due six weeks before your arrival, allowing you to plan your luxury holiday ibiza with peace of mind.
When is the best time to come to Ibiza?
For an extraordinary Ibiza experience, we suggest visiting during the peak season months of July and August. However, if you prefer quieter surroundings, May and September offer equally stunning options for a luxurious vacation in ibiza with fewer crowds.
What is the best location to book a villa?
We recommend booking villas within a 15-minute radius of Ibiza town to ensure easy access to clubs and restaurants, which enhances your stay. Alternatively, for a more relaxed retreat, consider villas in the serene north of the island.
When is the best time to prebook a villa?
Many villas accept bookings 1 or 2 years in advance and fill up very quickly. Nevertheless, we recommend booking your villa 6-4 months before arrival to ensure the best quality/price and availability for your comfortable and luxury vacation in Ibiza.
How do you ensure the privacy and security of guests during their stay
Many of our villas are equipped with professional alarm systems, providing an additional layer of protection and peace of mind. For those seeking enhanced security measures, we offer the option to add a private security for the villa, ensuring maximum safety throughout your stay.
